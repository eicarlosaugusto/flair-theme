## Refund policy / Política de reembolso

**Returns and refunds**

If you change your mind, you can cancel your order and return it for a refund.

**Changing your mind**
- You have **14 days from the day your order is delivered** to tell us you want to cancel.
- After telling us, you then have a further 14 days to send the item back.
- Please send items back unworn and in their original packaging. If an item has been handled more than needed to check it, we may reduce the refund to reflect any loss in value.
- Please contact us before sending anything back. We will reply with the return address and the steps to follow.
- Unless the item is faulty or not as described, return postage is paid by you. We recommend a tracked service, as we can't refund items that don't reach us.

**Pierced earrings**
For hygiene reasons, pierced earrings can't be returned once they have been taken out of their sealed packaging, unless they are faulty.

**Faulty or wrong items**
If your item arrives damaged, faulty or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 30 days of delivery with a photo. We will send a replacement or give you a full refund, including postage. After 30 days you still have your rights under the Consumer Rights Act 2015 to a repair, replacement or refund for faulty goods. This does not affect your statutory rights.

**Refunds**
We refund within 14 days of receiving the returned item, or of you showing us proof that you've sent it back, whichever is sooner. Refunds go to the payment method you used and cover the item price and the standard delivery charge you paid.

## Shipping policy / Política de frete

**Delivery**
- We deliver to addresses in the United Kingdom.
- Every order is sent with tracking. You'll get the tracking link by email once your order is dispatched.
- Delivery costs and the estimated delivery window are shown at checkout before you pay.
- Our pieces are sent from our suppliers' warehouses, so if you order more than one item they may arrive in separate parcels.
- All prices include VAT.

**Late or missing parcels**
If your tracking hasn't updated for 7 days, or your parcel hasn't arrived by the end of the estimated window, contact us and we'll chase it with the carrier. If it's lost, we'll send a replacement or refund you in full.
